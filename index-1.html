Common Types of Online Banking Scams

Phishing Attacks: Fraudsters send emails or messages that mimic your bank, asking you to click a link and enter your credentials. Always verify the sender's email address and never click suspicious links.

Fake Banking Apps: Some scammers create counterfeit banking apps that look identical to legitimate ones. Always download apps from official app stores and verify the developer information.

SIM Swapping: Criminals contact your mobile provider to transfer your phone number to their device, intercepting OTPs and gaining access to your accounts.

How to Protect Yourself

1. Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) â€” Add an extra layer of security beyond just your password.

2. Monitor Your Accounts Regularly â€” Check your transaction history frequently and set up real-time alerts for any activity.

3. Use Strong, Unique Passwords â€” Avoid using the same password across multiple platforms. Consider using a password manager.

4. Keep Software Updated â€” Ensure your device's operating system, browser, and banking apps are always up to date with the latest security patches.

5. Never Share OTPs or PINs â€” Your bank will never ask for these over phone, email, or text. If someone does, it's a scam.

What To Do If You're a Victim

If you suspect fraudulent activity, immediately contact your bank's helpline, freeze your account, change your passwords, and file a complaint with the cybercrime authorities. Quick action can minimize the damage significantly.
